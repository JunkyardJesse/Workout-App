Lift Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Lift Log does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only on your own device, inside the app. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file and share it yourself.

Notifications

The app asks permission to show a local notification when your rest timer finishes. These notifications are created on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact
